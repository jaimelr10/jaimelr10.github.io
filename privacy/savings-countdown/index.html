Privacy Policy — Savings Countdown

Last updated: 2026-08-19

Savings Countdown does not require registration or login. All data you enter is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any server owned by us.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to serve ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android)
  • IP address
  • Usage data

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized ads:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable for this app
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

Users in the European Economic Area are shown a consent dialog (Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are served.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The financial information you enter never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
